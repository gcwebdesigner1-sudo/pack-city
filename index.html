Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Pack City Pressure Washing is fully licensed and insured.

What's the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?

Pressure washing uses high-pressure water for hard surfaces like concrete and pavers. Soft washing uses low pressure and a cleaning solution for siding, stucco and painted surfaces that high pressure could damage.
