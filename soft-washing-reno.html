Soft washing and house washing in Reno

Stucco, siding and painted surfaces need cleaning, not blasting. Soft washing uses low pressure and the right cleaning solution to lift dirt, mildew and algae without damage.

Call (775) 560-4277 Text photos

Where soft washing is the right call

  • Stucco and painted exteriors
  • Vinyl and wood siding
  • Eaves, trim and soffits
  • Commercial building facades

Why not just pressure wash it?

High pressure can strip paint, force water behind siding and chip stucco. Soft washing lets the solution do the work, then rinses it away gently — the clean lasts longer because it treats the growth, not just the surface.

Questions about soft washing

Is soft washing safe for my plants?

Yes. We wet down landscaping before and rinse it after so plants and lawns aren't affected.

How long does a house wash take?

Most single-family homes are done in a few hours. Larger or commercial buildings depend on size and access.
