How the job goes

We use a commercial surface cleaner on flat concrete for an even, streak-free finish, then detail the edges and corners by hand. Wood and softer surfaces get lower pressure so nothing gets etched or splintered. You'll see the difference before we pack up.

Questions about pressure washing

How often should I pressure wash my driveway?

Most Reno homes look best with a cleaning once a year. Driveways with heavy oil drips, trees overhead or winter road grime may need it more often.

Can pressure washing damage concrete or pavers?

Too much pressure in the wrong hands can. We match the pressure and nozzle to the surface and use a surface cleaner on flat concrete to keep the finish even.
